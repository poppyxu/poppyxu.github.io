About me

I'm Poppy(Yingjing) Xu, currently an AI Researcher at ModelBest（面壁智能）, where my work focuses on post training of Omni Streaming-based Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM).

I received my Master's degree in Software Engineering from Zhejiang University, co-advised by Dr. Bo Lin and Prof. Jianwei Yin at the ACES-HiG Lab.

I'm fascinated by where AI is heading across tech, startups, and product design!
